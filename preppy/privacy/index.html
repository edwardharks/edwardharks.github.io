Preppy — Privacy Policy

Last updated 29 September 2026 · Little Bird Solutions

The short version. Preppy stores your Profile, completed Interviews and Scorecards on your device. When you practise, it sends relevant Profile details and interview text to our service. Our service uses OpenAI to create questions and feedback.

We record AI request and response content, including your Answers, in a dedicated PostHog project for quality review and troubleshooting. AI input and output content is configured for 30-day retention. We also use Google Firebase for Android usage and crash reporting, and Google Play and RevenueCat to manage subscriptions.

Please avoid including information about yourself or others that you are not comfortable sending to these providers.

1. Who is responsible

Preppy is provided by Little Bird Solutions ("we", "us" and "our"), which decides how the data described in this policy is used. This policy covers the Preppy Android app (solutions.littlebird.interviewprep) and its interview service. Contact us at contact@littlebirdsolutions.com.

2. What we collect and why

DataUse
Profile and practice contentYour industry, role, experience level, target companies, optional tailoring note, selected Question, Answers, conversation transcript and AI-generated Scorecard. We use this to tailor and deliver interview practice and review the quality of AI responses.
Device-linked service identityThe app uses an app-scoped Android device identifier to register with our service. We derive a pseudonymous service ID from it to manage the free Question, subscription access, retry protection, security and usage limits. We do not ask you to provide a name, email or password to use the app.
Purchase informationGoogle Play and RevenueCat process subscription purchases. We receive purchaser and entitlement status, such as whether access is active and when it expires. We do not receive your payment card number.
Usage and technical dataOn Android, Firebase Analytics records app events and screen use, with limited categories such as experience level. Firebase Crashlytics records crash and diagnostic information. These services also collect app-instance or installation IDs, device and app details, and approximate location inferred from network information. Our service and PostHog record pseudonymous IDs, operation type, result, latency and model information.

The Android app uses Firebase App Check and Google Play Integrity to check that requests come from a genuine app installation. These services process app and device integrity information to prevent misuse. Network requests to our service also carry technical information such as IP address and request time.

3. Interview content and other providers

The Profile and interview text needed for a Question, follow-up or Scorecard travel over HTTPS to our service, then to OpenAI for generation. Our service also sends the request and AI response to a dedicated PostHog project for quality review, cost monitoring and troubleshooting. These PostHog AI traces can include your Profile tailoring details, Answers, conversation and Scorecard. This applies to the free Question as well as subscription practice; there is no separate opt-in for AI traces.

Our service runs on Google Cloud in London and stores pseudonymous access, purchase and usage records in Firestore. It keeps a short-lived copy of some AI results to make retries safe. Ordinary service logs are intended to contain technical outcomes rather than interview text. Completed Interview History and Scorecards are stored on your device and are not synchronised to our service as a durable History archive.

Answers can mention employers, colleagues or sensitive projects. Do not submit information you are not entitled to share. AI-generated feedback may be inaccurate.

4. Analytics and crash reports

Firebase Analytics helps us understand onboarding and interview use. App-defined events use fixed categories and counts rather than Answer text or free-text Profile fields. Crashlytics helps us diagnose faults; our handled-error reporting removes error messages before sending them, although automatically captured crash reports can still include device and app state. Firebase also collects information through its SDKs independently of the events we define. On Android, the Analytics SDK can collect the advertising ID by default, along with its app-instance ID and device information. Preppy has no in-app analytics switch. We do not display ads or sell interview content.

5. How long data is kept

  • On your device: Profile, completed History, Scorecards and app settings remain until you clear the relevant data or uninstall the app. Android Backup is disabled for Preppy.
  • Retry cache: AI result copies stop being served after about ten minutes. Firestore's automatic deletion may remove an expired copy later.
  • PostHog: AI input and output content is configured to be removed after 30 days. Less detailed event metadata, such as pseudonymous IDs, operation, timing and outcome, may remain for the project's separate event-retention period.
  • Service records: Pseudonymous access, purchase-event and usage records remain while needed to operate and protect the service, unless we delete them following a valid request or a legal retention obligation applies. They do not form a durable archive of completed interview text.
  • Other providers: Firebase, RevenueCat, Google Play and OpenAI keep information under their applicable service settings and retention rules.

6. Your controls and deletion requests

You can edit your Profile in the app. Settings → Clear history removes completed Interviews and Scorecards from that device. Uninstalling removes the app's local data. These actions do not cancel a subscription or erase data that was already sent to our service or its providers. To manage or cancel a Google Play subscription, use Google Play subscriptions or Preppy's Plan settings.

To request access to or deletion of service data, email contact@littlebirdsolutions.com. Because Preppy has no sign-in or email account, we may need information from your app installation or purchase record to locate the right pseudonymous records and protect someone else's data. We will explain what we can identify and delete, including data held by service providers, any records we cannot reliably identify, and what must be retained for legal or security reasons. A deletion request does not itself cancel a store subscription.

7. Legal grounds and your rights

Where UK or European data protection law applies, we process data needed to deliver interview practice and subscription access to perform our agreement with you. We process service security information, AI quality traces, usage information and diagnostics for our legitimate interests in protecting and improving Preppy. These interests must be balanced against your rights and the sensitivity of information you choose to enter. Other consent or local-law requirements may also apply to particular analytics technologies.

You may have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to processing of personal data, and to receive a copy of data you provided. Contact us at the address above. You can also complain to your local data protection authority; in the UK this is the Information Commissioner's Office.

8. International processing

Our Google Cloud service is located in the UK and the dedicated PostHog project uses its EU endpoint. Other providers may process data outside your country, including outside the UK and EEA, under their applicable transfer arrangements. Contact us if you need more information about these arrangements.

9. Children and changes

Preppy is intended for people preparing for job interviews. It is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us.

We will update the date above when this policy changes and give appropriate notice of material changes. We will update it before any later iOS release to reflect that app's actual data flows.
